# CORE: Collaborative Optimization with Reinforcement Learning and Evolutionary Algorithm for Floorplanning

> **TL;DR:** CORE combines a neural B*-tree constructor with classical evolutionary search. Neural layouts enter an evolutionary population, while better evolutionary layouts supervise the constructor through imitation. The method improves the displayed floorplanning benchmarks, with substantial per-circuit optimization time and inconsistent printed n300 figures that affect its headline percentage.

## Motivation

Compact floorplans need a suitable structural representation and sufficient exploration. B*-trees encode non-overlapping compact placements; evolution explores complete layouts, while PPO learns sequential placement choices from task feedback. This is a floorplanning study, not evidence that the same method solves congestion-aware macro placement or complete chip design. [pp.1–4]

## Contributions

- Clustering-based Diversified Evolutionary Search(CDES) selects quality/diversity elites within coordinate-feature clusters.
- A B*-tree MDP permits learned module insertion, target-parent selection and rotation/left-right decisions.
- Reinforcement-driven population injection and evolution-guided imitation connect the two search processes.
- MCNC/GSRC comparisons, ablations, five-run summaries and selected parallel runtime measurements evaluate the hybrid. [pp.5–10,14–20]

## Methodology

**Evolution.** Start with a population of complete layouts. K-means clusters coordinate features; fitness mixes layout quality with novelty, defined from the two highest cosine similarities to other layouts. Each cluster retains elites and generates variants through rotation, module exchange and delete/reinsert mutations. The proposed population settings are100 layouts, four clusters, two elites per cluster and quality weightα=.8. These heuristic mechanisms encourage diversity; they do not guarantee escape from every local optimum. [pp.5–6,16–19]

**Neural construction.** PPO constructs a B*-tree from scratch. The initial module/root rotation is sampled; remaining modules are inserted one at a time. The policy conditions its three masked decisions on module/port/netlist features. A separate critic estimates state value. The formal architecture uses two SAGEConv layers and two two-head Transformer layers,64-dimensional representations and small action MLPs. The main reward is−10⁻⁴ΔHPWL plus optionalΔarea-utilization; unplaced nets are treated according to the current partial layout. [pp.6–7,15–17]

**Collaboration.** Complete layouts generated by PPO compete for evolutionary-population slots. The archive keeps superior layouts for the same root/rotation; their B*-tree construction sequences form imitation examples when they outperform the current policy. Thus evolution improves the training signal for a constructor, rather than a neural move policy receiving an entire incumbent and editing it. [pp.8,14–15]

**Classification:** Specialist / constructive, corrected from the old improvement label. Neural inference builds a layout from an empty tree; complete-layout mutations are classical. Training/search is circuit-specific and does not demonstrate one pretrained cross-problem generalist model. [pp.5–8,17]

## Limitations

### Curator Notes

- **Guarantees:** The checklist explicitly says this is empirical work without formal proofs. The background claim that clipped PPO guarantees monotonic improvement/convergence is stronger than what this paper establishes. Neither clipping nor population diversity proves global optimality of CORE.
- **Reward versions:** Main text uses differences in HPWL/utilization, while AppendixA.4 prints instantaneous−HPWL+utilization. The current worker code stores the difference between consecutive weighted scores, supporting the main formulation. With discount below1, this shaped return is not simply an undiscounted telescoping final objective.
- **Current release:** The example HPWL launcher uses L_weight1.0, discount default.999, varying6–90 rollout workers and default10K outer epochs, whereas formal settings use HPWL scale10⁻⁴, discount.99,30 completed layouts and20K epochs on the two largest cases. Defaults/scripts are not automatically exact reproductions of the published runs.
- **Implementation details:** The inspected release normalizes quality and computes cosine novelty over the current population before per-cluster elite selection, rather than recomputing novelty solely inside each cluster. It augments representations with raw features and uses SiLU in action heads, differing from the simplified64-dimensional/ReLU formal description. The inspected imitation guard requires strict improvement in both HPWL and utilization; these version details matter when running a pure-HPWL variant.
- Eight benchmark circuits and repeated optimization of those circuits do not demonstrate unseen-design transfer or industry-wide scale. Assumed baseline utilization, best-of-five tables, distinct objectives and unresolved numerical entries limit claims of universal dominance. No complete training run or chip-design benchmark was independently replayed here.
